# Decisions

Append-only. One entry per decision that would be expensive to reconstruct:
what was decided, why, and what it produced. Date every entry. A decision made
in conversation and not written here is lost at the end of the session.

Format:

```text
## <date> — <decision in one line>

**Why.** <the reasoning, including what was rejected>
**Artifacts.** <files this produced or changed>
```

# Human Gate Log

Every judgment gate the author resolved, with the options that were on the table
and the reason for the choice. Appended by `/gate-log`, one entry per decision.

This file is the reliability lever and the documentation exhibit at the same time. The call
asks discussants to assess "the nature of the human labor embodied in each
paper," and a decision recorded only in a conversation cannot be assessed. The
pipeline's six gates are G1 idea, G2 framing, G3 design freeze, G4 data calls,
G5 what survives, and G6 title and overclaim.

Each entry carries: the gate, the date, the options considered, the choice, the
rationale, who made it, **the review packet it was read from**, and links to the
artifacts it turned on. Record the options that were **rejected** — an entry
naming only what was chosen documents an outcome rather than a judgment.

**Read from** names the PDF the author actually read. Every gate is presented as
one rendered packet rather than a set of Markdown files (author decision
), and a discussant assessing the nature of the human labour is asking
what was in front of the person when they decided. A record pointing at source
files the author never opened answers a different question.

Where a decision was later reversed or corrected, append the correction as its
own entry rather than editing the original. A superseded decision stays
readable; that is what makes the record auditable rather than tidy.

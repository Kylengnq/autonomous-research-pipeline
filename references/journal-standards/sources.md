## Sources sought and not found (do not fabricate)

- A JFE "report of the editor" with desk-rejection and R&R rates: JFE does not
  publish one. All JFE editorial-statistics claims are marked LOW or omitted.
- An RFS desk-rejection-rate figure: the SFS statistics page (S9) reports
  acceptance rates and turnaround but not the desk-reject share. The dossier
  infers only what S8/S9 state (fast first decisions driven by desk rejects).
- Welch "writing advice for authors": Welch's paper (S13) is about referee
  behavior, not author craft; it is used only for referee-disagreement claims.
